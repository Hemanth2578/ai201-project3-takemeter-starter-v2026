Business: News about companies or businesses, including transactions, restructuring, mergers, credit ratings, analyst ratings, stock forecasts, and financial fraud involving companies or money, plus stock, commodity-futures and crypto price moves.
Economy: News about real-world economic conditions of a country, state, or the world, such as supply and production, trade, jobs, wages, cost of living and inflation.
Administrative: Actions or measures taken by an agency, such as a government office or public official, a nonprofit, a community organization, or an HOA.
None of These: An article that reports no company news, no economic conditions and no agency action, such as elections and campaigns, a story about one individual, or a non-finance topic like sports, entertainment or gardening.
